# Our Memory Garden

A shared garden for presenting and preserving the couple's stories and photos.

## Language

**Page**:
A collection of arranged content in the garden, visible either to everyone or only to the two administrators.

**Template**:
A starting arrangement for a new page. Once created, the page can be changed independently of its template.

**Block**:
A piece of page content, such as text, a banner, or a photo grid, that can be arranged with other blocks.

**Administrator**:
One of the two people who can manage the garden and view its private pages.
